# Brand direction: vert-ui

- Name: **vert-ui**
- Tagline: "Grow with precision"
- Concept: calm, fresh, green-accented components with soft motion
- Personality: minimal, airy, natural, precise
- Target: indie hackers, startups, dashboard builders

## Logo idea
Geometric "V" (vert) stylized as an upward arrow representing growth. Accent the top vertex with emerald/lime glow. Use clean geometric sans, minimal monoline with soft rounded terminals. Avoid leafy/eco clichés; focus on "growth + precision".

## Palette (vert primary)
- Primary (vert): emerald #10b981, lime #84cc16, chartreuse forward accent #bef264
- Cool neutrals: gray-950–50 for crisp contrast; neutral slate undertones for airy feel
- Accents: soft emerald glow for borders, subtle grain gradient as signature

## Signature element
"Soft grain gradients + thin luminous emerald borders" with generous radius (rounded-3xl). Motion subtle and natural.
